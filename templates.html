📋 Message Templates

Create and manage your Meta-approved WhatsApp templates. All bulk campaigns require an approved template.

How it works: Submit a template → Meta reviews it (usually minutes to a few hours) → Once Approved, use it in your campaigns. Provide sample values for all variables ({{1}}, {{2}}) — Meta uses these to review your content. Templates that are vague, promotional without opt-out, or missing samples get rejected.
